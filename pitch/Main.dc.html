02 · The problem

Liquidation is DeFi's most important safety mechanism and its most extractive one.

310,000+ liquidations processed by Aave, worth ~$4.65B Aave Labs, Feb 2026 · [TBD link]
~$429M liquidated by Aave in the crash week of Jan 31 – Feb 5, 2026, across ~12,500 transactions Aave Labs, Feb 2026 · [TBD link]
~5% of the debt paid as a bonus to outside bots, at the worst moment for the borrower
74% of fixed-spread liquidations paid above-average transaction fees: evidence of gas bidding wars Qin et al., 2021

Existing designs “sell excessive amounts of discounted collateral at the borrowers' expense.”

Qin et al., “An Empirical Study of DeFi Liquidations”, 2021
03 · The insight

“Liquidators were a workaround for loans that couldn't act on their own.”

A liquidator does six jobs. Autolysis gives each one to the market itself, or to someone already paid for it.

#Liquidator's jobWhat replaces it in Autolysis
1Watch positionsAnyone can broadcast; it costs nothing, because the account pays gas.
2Sign and sendNo key; the account's VERIFY frame is the authorization.
3Bring capitalNot needed; the market sells collateral it already holds.
4Pay gasPrefunded ETH tank, refilled from collateral, capped at 0.3% of what's sold.
5Hold collateral and price riskAqua makers and a backstop buy at the oracle price for a spread.
6Act fastLiquidatable from HF < 1; one nonce key per position, so many can be pending at once.

Formulas
Aave v3: if HF ≥ 0.95 repay 50% of D, else 100%. Paid = 5% of repaid.
Aave v4: r = (T·D − CP·L) / (T − L·1.04). Paid = 4% of r.
Autolysis: r = (T·D − CP·L) / (T − L/(1−s)). Sold = r/(1−s). Cost = sold × (0.2% to 1.1%): maker spread 0.2–0.8% plus gas capped at 0.3%.

Costs move, they don't disappear: borrowers still pay gas, the spread and slippage, just not the bonus.

09 · FAQ

Questions a skeptical judge should ask.

WhyWhy does this need to exist?

Liquidation is DeFi's most important safety mechanism and its most extractive one. Aave has processed 310,000+ liquidations worth ~$4.65B, and outside bots are paid a bonus of about 5% of the debt, at the worst moment for the borrower.

Qin et al. (2021) found 74% of fixed-spread liquidations paid above-average transaction fees, and that existing designs “sell excessive amounts of discounted collateral at the borrowers' expense.”

WhyWhy not just keep liquidators?

Liquidators were a workaround for loans that couldn't act on their own. Each of their six jobs (watch, sign, bring capital, pay gas, hold price risk, act fast) has a replacement inside the market; see the insight table. The bonus pays for work the market can now do itself.

AuthorizeIf nobody gets a bonus, who sends the transaction?

Anyone. Broadcasting costs nothing, because the account pays. The borrower's app, a watcher or any node can do it; builders are paid by the priority fee. So: no privileged keeper, not no keeper.

CostSo liquidation is free?

No. Gas, spread and slippage remain; we quote net savings. Autolysis removes most of the 5%, net. Try the cost calculator.

AuthorizeCan collateral pay its own gas?

Not in the same transaction. EIP-8141 charges the max cost at approval, so the account keeps a prefunded tank, refilled from collateral afterwards.

FundCan someone sandwich it?

The Aqua and backstop tiers fill at the oracle price. Only the curve tier can be moved, and at most to the 1% minOut floor.

AuthorizeWhat if the alt mempool stalls in a crash?

Lender fallback: below HF 0.95, after 3 rounds with no self-liquidation, a classic 5% liquidation opens to anyone. An invariant proves it never pre-empts self-liquidation.

AuthorizeCan someone grief the account into paying for failed attempts?

VERIFY checks the round, the band, two-price health and the fee cap. At most one failed attempt is billed per price round per position.

AuthorizeWhy not ERC-4337?

Autolysis is built on EIP-8141 frame transactions: the account's VERIFY frame is the authorization and the account is both sender and gas payer, so no key signs. Its validation reads market and oracle storage, which the ERC-8421 alternative mempool admits from a staked account.

[TBD] Direct comparison with ERC-4337.

ExecuteWhy a hook and not a router?

The hook sits inside the official PoolManager's swap path. BackstopHook authorizes on the swap's sender (never hookData) and uses beforeSwapReturnDelta to fill at the oracle price, while everyone else sees an ordinary pool.

FundWhy Aqua?

Aqua makers' USDC stays in their wallets, and one balance backs every market. Our LiquidationGate opcode lets an order fill only when the hook is the taker during a liquidation, so makers quote at the oracle price for a spread without exposing their orders to anyone else.

AuthorizeOracle wicks?

The position must be unhealthy at both the cited and the latest round, within a 0.5% band. A breaker needs two rounds for moves over 10%.

AuthorizeWhat is EXO-010?

A proposed rule (EXO-010): a narrowly scoped ERC-8421 reputation rule. An invalidation caused by an outside state change the sender didn't cause, such as an oracle round moving or the borrower repaying, shouldn't count against the sender. Malformed or self-caused invalidations still count.

StatusIs it live on mainnet?

No. EIP-8141 and ERC-8421 are drafts on an experimental devnet. The Uniswap and Aqua parts run against official contracts on a Unichain mainnet fork.

AdoptionWould this work with Aave or Morpho?

Autolysis is a liquidation route, not a new lending protocol. Any market implementing ISelfLiquidatingMarket can adopt it. Our demo market is deliberately minimal.

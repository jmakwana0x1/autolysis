Does the bounce rule throttle honest carriers?

A crash opens 150 pending liquidations; then the oracle bounces back and invalidates them all at once. Under today's ERC-8421 rule every invalidated pending still counts toward the carrier's seen count, pushing max_seen (= seen / 10) past the throttle line. The proposed EXO-010 rule decrements seen for a miss caused by an outside state change, so a market-wide bounce doesn't throttle honest carriers.
